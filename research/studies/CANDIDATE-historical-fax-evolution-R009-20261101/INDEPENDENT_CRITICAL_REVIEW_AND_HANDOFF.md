## Decision-relevant result

Gemini correctly treats the FAX universe as **an autonomously evolving, alternative globally popular squash sport**, not “PSA 2026 with more players”. Its most valuable contribution is a four-layer description: relatively stable individual style, opponent/context-specific gameplan, emergent distribution of successful tactical choices (“meta”), and innovation that may alter opportunities or performance tradeoffs. It also separates fixed historically versioned exogenous World Package inputs from hypothetical endogenous responses.

**The report repeatedly upgrades plausible hypotheses into scientific certainties or already implemented engine mechanics.** In particular, forced three-style counter cycles, universal Top-100 compression, injury risk feedback, and specific time lags are not established by squash evidence. Do not implement its replicator-mutator, institutional diffusion or EVT numerical formulae as pre-alpha decisions.

### General scientific caution

- The study calls observational differences in winners/unforced errors and rest times **causal consequences** of scoring changes; concurrent equipment, tin, era, selected match cohorts, training and tournament conditions can confound the effect. “Different means” is not an RCT. Critical points and match duration can also change mechanically via scoring, so separate tactical adaptation from rules arithmetic.
- The article's physiological/statistical rates refer to *particular* male elite, mixed elite and adolescent populations. The R005 independent audit already rejected use of Rejeb/Horsley source statistics as adult-male hazard/severity distributions. R009 reuses some of those figures in its figure on p.14 and text; they must never become FAX health parameters by citation repetition.
- The document's time-dependent equations are presented in text with missing or dropped Math objects. Explicitly consult original DOCX graphics before accepting formula signs, units, normalizers, mutational simplex conservation or bounds. A formula producing visually attractive cycles is not validated by citation alone.

## What should be retained for the alternate global FAX project

1. **Separating individual style, match Gameplan, population meta and innovation** provides a useful conceptual model and suggests better *descriptive* analytics without needing new gameplay.
2. **Environments, opportunities and technology can change over time** through historically source-owned World Package events; **performance change need not be monotone**, and some biological/geometry constraints persist conditionally.
3. **More global players increases possibilities, not mandated future champions**, points or parity. FAX might naturally have deeper fields, long-lived hegemonies, innovation plateaus and differences in tactics.
4. **Never author innate potential/style/mentality by nationality.** Country differences may affect probabilities of accessing/reaching elite levels through allowed participation, development and support inputs.
5. **Observed Earth PSA stats are evidence for mechanisms and regime-specific empirical bounds, not FAX's required historical aggregate trajectory.** Explicitly separate Earth observation / FAX counterfactual assumption / simulated outcome / desired user narrative.
6. **R007 × R008 should precede calibration:** check source provenance/sex/era/license, define meaningful observables/falsification, account for real computed per-Run calendar W (Earth 52, reference FAX 61) and historically versioned competition rules.
7. **No need to script tactical eras**. At minimum observe the existing Gameplan/R006 population outcomes over multiple seeds and document their variability. *If* an evolution mechanic is later authorized, test the simplest conservative school/academy mechanism against null/delayed controls; oscillations are possible but not required.
