# Earth source research — incomplete

These are source catalogues and extracted observations, not usable World Packages.
Both `countries` maps remain empty. Do not fill missing research with neutral scores
or present modern surveys as measured history for 1955–2026.

The catalogues now use `earth_sport_research.v2`. Populated rating evidence must
cite exact normalized observation IDs from registered, content-addressed
`earth_source_observations.v2` datasets.

The existing files in `observations/` predate that normalized contract. They remain
raw/source-specific research inputs until explicitly converted; they must not be
silently treated as comparable measurements or referenced by new rating histories.

See [the implementation/research boundary](../../docs/EARTH_SPORT_HISTORY_V1.md)
for source limitations, provenance rules, audit/build commands and next steps.
