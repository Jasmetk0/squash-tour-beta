## Main decision

**Accept R009's *epistemology and falsifiable hypothesis framing*, not its preselected historical outcomes or proposed mechanics as required.**

An alternative, globally popular FAX squash world is **not Earth PSA 2026 scaled up**. A credible FAX world evolves because versioned rules, real country conditions, talent access, player development, match tactical interactions, resource constraints, choices and later explicitly authorized technology/institutional mechanisms can produce different histories.

An emergent output is allowed to include: strong dynasties or none, tactical shifts or stable meta, improving absolute player skill or plateau, country concentration or geographical spread. **Do not force an Egypt/Pakistan analog, particular tactical period, nationalism or an arbitrary skill-inflation trend.**

## Credible scientific ideas worth retaining

1. Four-layer taxonomy: **individual Player Style → context-specific Match Gameplan → observed population tactical meta → separately defined historical innovation**. These are different causal/temporal levels; one does not prove an implementation of the next.
2. Rules/scoring, court geometry, equipment and participation are legitimate **historical conditions**, but old PSA event dates and observational changes are NOT mandatory FAX event timings or effect sizes.
3. Climate of coaching/academy access, squash participation and support can affect *the number and conversion of potential prospects*, without any national innate potential bonus.
4. Increasing effective pool is a conditional sampling hypothesis. Absolute physical capability, realized sport performance, opponent-relative strength and published ranking must be measured **separately**.
5. Real history of Pakistani and Egyptian dominance demonstrates that such concentrated social/institutional contexts are possible; it does not prove an Egypt-style dynasty must exist in every plausible world.
6. Highly valuable **non-creation tests:** no nationality direct-talent wiring; deterministic reproducibility; history-snapshot provenance; current Gameplan axis/local strength decomposition; explicit distinction between calculated and implemented health effects.

## Assessment of original 15 experiments

The ChatGPT audit systematically reviewed all **EXP-01..EXP-15**. No experiments were executed and no new validation gate was accepted.

### Highest-value now / when not duplicated by existing PRs

- **EXP-12 / revised A2:** Permute nationality *label* while holding the genuine player/endogenous environment inputs and semantic RNG identities fixed; assert no direct innate-potential/style effect. A statistical failure to reject (`p>0.05`) is not a valid proof. Ensure real country environment still legitimately influences player pool and development.
- **EXP-02 / revised A1:** Controlled effective-pool sampling, distribution-family-aware order statistics. Measure latent potential separately from realized skill/ranking.
- **EXP-09 / revised A5:** Local Gameplan causal response with score/workload/control/effort mediators; coordinate **R006 #1714** legacy archetype/momentum concerns, do not delete effects by intuition.
- **EXP-13 / revised A10:** Exact Save/Load/restart and historical reproducibility; check overlap with **#1713/#1718**; these current Draft integrations are not proof tests already passed.
- **EXP-08 / revised A3:** Country development mediation via effective pool and permitted environment only; ensure six authored ratings and explicit true source snapshots.

### Defer or reject as immediate pre-alpha tasks

- **EXP-01/03/04/05/06:** 50-year stationarity ADF, coaching networks, champion imitation and forced 10–25-year RPS/Hopf cycles are not implemented, properly calibrated or compulsory.
- **EXP-07/10:** Long dynasty/ranking historical distributions need complete career, Tour, ranking and multi-seed observability; no 15–30 year quota.
- **EXP-11:** Frozen-era champion clone stress comparison useful later but no arbitrary 45–55% win requirement against opponents whose world-era equipment/Rules/skill may legitimately differ.
- **EXP-14:** Identifiability requires parameter-to-observable map, multiple synthetic recovery datasets and condition-specific assumptions; correlation matrix alone cannot decide identifiable vs unidentifiable.
- **EXP-15:** Massive 100-year campaigns depend on runtime, memory, Save durability and supported dynamic features; zero failures among 50 is not proof of universal rare-event safety.

### The ChatGPT audit's revised 10 candidate tests

A1 Population sampling law; A2 Country-label invariance; A3 Country pipeline mediation; A4 Development saturation; A5 Gameplan causal response; A6 Emergent matchup topology; A7 Meta without forced cycles; A8 National concentration; A9 RAE institutional selection; A10 Save/replay and rare failures.

Only A1/A2 and parts of A3/A5/A10 may be immediately feasible after checking latest implementation and PR overlap. **A6–A9 are largely conditional future-model science**, not a commitment to build those models.

## Integration with R001–R008

- **R001 talent cohorts**: distinguish junior sampling vs later achieved senior performance. Historical self-contained cohorts are necessary before claiming late-bloomer distributions.
- **R002 match realism**: validate score distributions *conditional on true effective strength and rule era*, not Earth tour overall average.
- **R003 ranking**: rankings are outcomes; success concentration and dynasty duration are not inputs/quotas.
- **R004 tournament Entry**: the choice set and ranking sources must reflect deadline-known facts only. Different future FAX environments can produce different Entry distributions legitimately.
- **R005 Health**: stored Injury/Illness ≠ calibrated automatic injury hazard or Return to Performance probability; age/match fatigue must not be counted twice.
- **R006 tactics**: no empirically proven fixed counter-cycle and no forced momentum; assess 4 continuous Gameplan axes, style familiarity and existing legacy pathways.
